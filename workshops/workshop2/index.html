Step-by-Step Task Guide

Workshop Tasks & Completion Instructions

Follow these instructions step-by-step to fill in the # TODO: exercise blocks in your notebook and train your reinforcement learning agent.

TASK 1

Explore Environment & State-Action Space (Parts 1 & 2)

Run Parts 1 & 2 of the notebook to examine the Pac-Man environment:

  • Inspect the 7x7 grid maze layout, pellet locations, and ghost initial position.
  • Observe the random baseline agent to see pure exploration (zero exploitation) before any Q-learning takes place.
TASK 2

Review Hyperparameters & Reward Shaping (Part 3)

Inspect the hyperparameter table controlling training dynamics:

  • ALPHA: Learning rate determining update step size.
  • GAMMA: Discount factor weighting immediate vs. future rewards.
  • EPSILON_START / EPSILON_MIN / EPSILON_DECAY: Exploration decay schedule.
  • PELLET_REWARD, GHOST_PENALTY, WIN_REWARD, STEP_PENALTY: Reward shaping parameters.
TASK 3

Implement Epsilon-Greedy Exploitation Branch (Part 4 - TODO)

Locate Part 4 in the notebook and complete the exploitation branch of the Epsilon-Greedy policy: 

# TODO: Return the action index with the highest Q-value for the current state

TASK 4

Implement the Bellman Q-Learning Update Equation (Part 5 - TODO)

Locate Part 5 in the training loop and fill in the Bellman update rule: 

# TODO: Calculate best_next_q, target, and update q_table[state][action]
best_next_q = 0 if done else np.max(q_table[next_state])
target = reward + GAMMA * best_next_q
q_table[state][action] += ALPHA * (target - q_table[state][action])
TASK 5

Train Agent & Plot Learning Curves (Parts 6, 7 & 8)

Train your Q-learning model across 1,000+ episodes and evaluate performance:

  • Execute the training loop and observe win rate climbing.
  • Plot total reward per episode and rolling averages to verify convergence.
  • Set epsilon = 0 to test your fully trained policy in action.
TASK 6

Conduct Hyperparameter Experiments (Part 9)

Experiment with at least 2-3 hyperparameter variations in Part 9:

  • Test fast decay (EPSILON_DECAY = 0.99) vs. slow decay (0.9999).
  • Compare short-sighted (GAMMA = 0.1) vs. far-sighted (GAMMA = 0.99) agents.
  • Adjust reward shaping parameters (STEP_PENALTY, GHOST_PENALTY) to analyze behavioral changes.
